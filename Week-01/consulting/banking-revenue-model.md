Net Interest Margin
You deposit $100,000 with CommBank. They pay you 2% interest, so they owe you $2,000 a year.

They lend that same $100,000 to someone buying a house. They charge 6%, so the borrower pays them $6,000 a year.

CommBank collected $6,000 and paid out $2,000.

They kept $4,000.

That $4,000 is net interest income. Money in from lending, minus money out to funders.

$4,000 kept, on $100,000 of lending.

$4,000 ÷ $100,000 = 4%

That percentage is the net interest margin. It's the profit margin on the money they move.
